# NNNN: Decision in a few words

- **Status:** proposed | decided | superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decided by:** names
- **Review date:** when to check the outcome
- **Features:** F-xx, or none

## Question

What we had to decide, in one or two sentences.

## Context

The facts and constraints that mattered, with links to evidence (issues, PRs, docs). No raw interview data or Slack messages; this repo is public.

## Options

### Chosen: option name

What it is, and why it won.

### Pruned: option name

What it is, and why it lost. Keep every serious option here, even the obvious losers.

## Consequences

What this commits us to, and what becomes harder.

## Outcome

Fill this in on the review date: what happened, and would we decide the same way again?
